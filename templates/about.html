About Foodie

A recipe scaler and weekly meal planner for people who want to eat well without spreadsheet math. Discover dishes, adjust servings, and map one meal per day across your week.

How it works

1

Search & open a recipe

Use Discover recipes to find meals from the Edamam database. Each card shows calories and macros per serving.

2

Scale portions

Change the serving count on a recipe page and ingredient quantities update automatically so shopping lists stay accurate.

3

Plan your week

Log in and assign one recipe per weekday on your meal plan. Replace or clear a day anytime—your plan is saved to your account.

What you can do today
  • Recipe search with nutrition summaries (Edamam Recipe Search API)
  • Servings-based scaling for ingredients on the recipe detail page
  • Weekly planner: Monday through Sunday, one slot per day
  • Calories, protein, carbs, and fat displayed per recipe
  • Account registration and password login
  • Sign in with GitHub (optional OAuth)
  • Remember me for longer sessions on trusted devices
  • Recipes cached in our database to reduce repeat API calls

Technology

Python Flask app with PostgreSQL, Jinja templates, and vanilla JavaScript. Production deployments use Gunicorn behind nginx or a platform router (e.g. Render).

Recipe and nutrition data come from the Edamam Recipe Search API. Free-tier limits apply—best suited for demos and small teams.

Privacy & accounts

Your meal plan and login are tied to your Foodie account. We do not ask end users for their own Edamam keys—the server uses one shared developer credential.

Do not share passwords in public channels. For local development, copy .env.example to .env and keep secrets out of git.
